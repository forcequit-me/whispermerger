Speech models

Turns audio into text. Download ahead of time, or a run downloads what it needs (with progress you can cancel). A canceled or dropped download resumes where it stopped.

Word-timing models

One per language. Pins every word to its exact time for subtitles and per-line playback. A language without one still transcribes, with line timings only.
